BUILDING 1P — DESIGN STUDY, PHASE 1
Option C — "long-span" apartments (3 bedrooms + kitchenette)

WHAT THIS IS
A massing and layout study, NOT construction documentation. It was produced
to find the right proportions of areas and a workable layout before handing
the project to an architect/engineer. All drawings are generated from a
single parametric model, so they are mutually consistent — but dimensions,
structure and building services still need professional design and checking.

SITE AND ENVELOPE
  Site:              Kourimskeho street, Pelhrimov, Czech Republic
                     (new side road between the Optokon and Weha plants)
  Building type:     prefabricated hall, structural grid 7.0 m
  Phase 1 footprint: 18.0 x 28.0 m (full build-out 18.0 x 56.0 m)
  Eaves height:      6.00 m       Ridge: 7.59 m      Roof pitch: 10 deg
  Ground floor:      504 m2       First floor: 413 m2      Total: 917 m2
  Floor area ratio:  1.82x the footprint
  North wall:        SITE BOUNDARY — solid wall, no openings
  All entrances, the workshop gate and vehicle access are on the SOUTH side

ACCOMMODATION (option C)
  Ground floor: 2 apartments 49 m2 (1 bed + kitchenette), gym, sim-racing
                room, shared workshop (double height), storage, plant room
  First floor:  3 apartments approx. 84 m2 (3 bedrooms + kitchenette) running
                the full 18 m span, offices, meeting/training room, sanitary
                facilities, rentable reserve space
  Access to the upper apartments is external: an access balcony along the
  south facade with a single straight steel stair — the residential part
  never meets the company operation.
  The north wall is on the site boundary, so bedrooms and children's rooms
  are daylit by ROOF WINDOWS in the north roof plane (diffuse light, no
  conflict with the PV array, which is on the south plane only).

DRAWING CONVENTIONS
  Levels are given in metres relative to the ground floor (+/- 0.000).
  Elevations show what an observer standing outside that facade sees, so the
  SOUTH elevation has EAST on the right and the EAST elevation has SOUTH on
  the left.
  Openings in the elevations are generated from the same data as the 3D
  model, so the two cannot disagree about windows.
  Red lines in the plans are fire-separating structures; the hatched grey
  gable is the temporary wall to be removed when phase 2 is built.

MATERIALS AND APPEARANCE
  Anthracite facade panels throughout; the only accent colour is the main
  entrance portal (white frame with a lime-green reveal, company brand).
  The east gable is fully glazed up to eaves level with mullions at 1.5 m
  and oak brise-soleil over the southern third.
  Photovoltaics on the south roof plane; the north plane is currently off.

QUESTIONS THE STUDY DELIBERATELY LEAVES OPEN
  - Structural design, foundations and building services are indicative only.
  - Fire safety: escape routes and compartments are indicated, not certified.
    The residential units and the company operation are separate compartments
    in the model, but the whole scheme needs a fire safety report.
  - Rental figures and operating costs in the underlying model are estimates.
  - Roof windows serving habitable rooms need a daylight calculation.
  - Local planning: residential use is permitted in this zone (confirmed with
    the building authority); the mix of housing and light industry on one
    plot still needs to be agreed formally.
  - Acoustics between the workshop/gym and the apartments is not solved.
